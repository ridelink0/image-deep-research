# Privacy

Image deep research is instructions only. It runs no code, operates no
server, reads no credentials and collects nothing. Search words go only to
the public collections and websites the person asks Claude to search or open,
under those sites' own terms. Moodboards and notes stay in the folder the
person chooses. Claude processes the conversation under the person's Claude
plan's terms.

Issues: https://github.com/ridelink0/image-deep-research/issues
